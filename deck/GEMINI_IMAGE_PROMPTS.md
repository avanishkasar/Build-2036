# Image prompts for the ADHIKAR deck

Paste each prompt into Gemini (Imagen) and download the result at 16:9. Drag the image into the matching Canva slide and send it to the back, or use it as a picture frame fill.

Style rules for every image: dark navy background (#0B0F17), saffron amber accent (#F5A524), no text inside the image, no logos, and no real brand marks such as UPI, NPCI or bank logos.

1. **Cover (slide 1)**
   A cinematic dark navy scene of a glowing amber shield floating above a stream of tiny light particles flowing like payments across a stylised map of India at night. Subtle green and red light accents. Premium fintech style, high detail, soft volumetric light, no text.

2. **Problem (slide 2)**
   A friendly looking AI robot assistant holding a glowing phone, while a thin red thread from a shadowy web page pulls its hand towards a different direction. Dark navy background, amber and red highlights, clean 3D illustration style, no text.

3. **Solution (slide 3)**
   A sleek translucent gate made of amber light standing between a small AI robot on the left and a glowing payment stream on the right. One green light above the gate. Minimal 3D render, dark navy background, no text.

4. **Use case, Pithampur factory (slide 7)**
   A modern small Indian auto parts workshop at dusk, steel rods and cardboard packaging stacked neatly, a middle aged owner looking at a tablet showing a green check. Warm realistic photo style with amber light, shallow depth of field, no visible text or logos.

5. **Impact (slide 13)**
   A vibrant Indore street market at golden hour with small shop owners using phones to receive payments, faint amber light trails connecting the shops like a network. Realistic, warm, optimistic, no visible text or logos.

6. **Closing (slide 16)**
   A time capsule made of brushed metal with a soft amber glow, labelled only with the year 2036 engraved, resting on a dark stage with a single spotlight. Elegant, cinematic, minimal.
